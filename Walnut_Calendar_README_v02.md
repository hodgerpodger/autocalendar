# Walnut Automatic Calendar

A compact automatic mechanical desk calendar with a rounded walnut enclosure, three recessed physical display windows, and no electronic display. Shows **weekday · month · date** and advances automatically.

## Approved visual directions

| Light — Ivory | Dark — Charcoal |
|---|---|
| ![Light ivory](design/light-ivory.png) | ![Dark charcoal](design/dark-charcoal.png) |

Both use the **same Modern Contrast bold geometric sans-serif typography** and walnut housing. Only the display background/lettering colors differ.

## For engineering agents

Start by evaluating mechanically feasible ways to show one clean value per window with no neighboring dates visible. Propose options and constraints before committing to CAD or firmware. The renders are aesthetic targets, not mechanical blueprints.
